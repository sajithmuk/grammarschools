What may change in 2027 checked 10 October 2026

I found no officially announced change to test format for the 2027 sitting. Treat the points below as a watch list, not confirmed news.

  • Conflicting reports on QE Boys: some guides say it sets its own papers, others say it joins a London consortium assessment. Confirm on qebarnet.co.uk before registering.
  • Dates drift by a few days each year (SET was mid-September, boys' second stage the first Saturday of October).
  • Cohort size and applications are rising at the London super-selectives, so cut-offs tend to creep up.
  • Re-check: Kent Test writing-task rules, Bucks pass standard, Sutton second-stage content, Tiffin Stage 2 content. Schools publish the next cycle's admissions policy by spring.
  • Sutton grammars have published 2027 PAN: Sutton Grammar 150 places.

How to assess a school as a parent

  • Odds before prestige: QE Boys (about 9% success) and Sutton schools (up to 10 applicants per place) mean you need a spread of realistic choices.
  • Travel: an hour each way for seven years matters. Sutton, Kingston and Barnet are 20 to 25 miles from Ilford; Ilford County High is the only one on your doorstep.
  • Priority rules: Ilford County High and Kent/Bucks schools give local children priority; London super-selectives rank purely on score.
  • Visit open evenings (June to September) and ask about sixth-form entry criteria, Oxbridge/Russell Group progression, and bursary or transport support.
